🧠 Core Concept Being Tested

Arrhenius Equation & Activation Energy

📐 General Problem-Solving Approach

Apply Arrhenius equation: k = A·e^(-Ea/RT). Use ln(k₂/k₁) = (Ea/R)(1/T₁ - 1/T₂) for two-temperature problems. Activation energy from slope of ln k vs 1/T graph.

📋 Required Formulae

  • k = Ae^(-Ea/RT)
  • ln(k₂/k₁) = (Ea/R)(1/T₁ - 1/T₂)
