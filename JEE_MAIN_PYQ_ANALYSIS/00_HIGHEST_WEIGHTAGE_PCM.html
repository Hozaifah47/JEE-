7. Data Accuracy & Methodology

How These Numbers Were Calculated

  • Source: 90 MathonGo JEE Main papers (2021–2025), all available sessions and shifts
  • Extraction: PyMuPDF text extraction with regex-based question boundary detection
  • Classification: Keyword-based chapter matching against 73-chapter JEE Main taxonomy
  • Counting: Each question occurrence counted once per paper; no duplicate paper counting
  • Limitations: Keyword matching may misclassify some questions. 2062 questions flagged as "Unclassified" due to low confidence
  • Not predictions: These are historical occurrence counts, not guaranteed future weightage
