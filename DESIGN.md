# JEE Main PYQ Portal Design Direction

## Identity

This is a study tool for dense, repeated practice. Its character comes from clarity, dependable hierarchy, and the visual language of marked-up exam material—not from decorative effects.

## Theme

The portal uses a dark-first reading surface because long question sets, answer states, and source figures are the primary work. Practice pages may expose a light/dark preference; pages that are generated as fixed dark analysis views should keep the same neutral tokens and contrast relationships.

## Palette

- Neutral background: near-black (`#0d0d11`) for dense study surfaces.
- Surface: charcoal (`#18181c`) for question cards.
- Text: off-white primary and muted gray secondary text.
- Blue: navigation and primary actions only.
- Green/red: answer state only.
- Amber: priority state only.

## Typography and composition

Use the system sans stack for compact, reliable reading across devices. Question text gets the largest reading measure; metadata stays quiet. Tables and source figures are structural content, not decoration. Cards group questions and answer states, not arbitrary features.

## Interaction principles

Every control must have a real destination or state change. Answer feedback uses color plus text, and navigation must expose chapter-to-cluster relationships directly. Decorative emoji, repeated gradients, and ornamental badges are excluded unless they carry content meaning.

## Accessibility baseline

Maintain visible focus states, keyboard-reachable controls, readable contrast in both themes, descriptive image alt text, and live status text for asynchronous actions.
